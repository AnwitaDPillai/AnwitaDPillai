## ABOUT

> *I read footnotes for fun and hold grudges on behalf of dead people.*

I got into archives because I was nosy about the wrong things. That curiosity turned into a book-length project, a pile of competition certificates, and bylines that still feel like a prank someone played on my life. I'm currently building a Substack because I believe rigorous history should be as readable as a good grudge, and finishing a monograph because I started it and I'm stubborn.

I also write poetry, which is just history with the citations removed.

## HOW I GOT HERE

Self-taught with no paid programs or institutional support. I wrote my first stories on a secondhand computer running Windows 11 on aging hardware. I didn't have WiFi until age 12; I downloaded entire philosophy libraries in single nights when the connection held and reverse-engineered winning essays like source code. The ~₹70,000 earned from international writing competitions sits in a bank account I opened at 15. 

The constraints were never obstacles. They were the forge.
